# Example prompts

Paste any of these into Cursor (Agent) after the GrowFast server shows a green dot in Customize, then MCPs. None of them sends anything.

1. `Use GrowFast to scan growfast.sh and tell me who its ideal customers are. Show the 5 best-fit companies with their scores and reasons.`
2. `Draft a first email to the founders of the top 2 companies from that scan. Don't send anything.`
3. `Which products am I listening for on Reddit with GrowFast?`
4. `Show me the newest Reddit posts GrowFast found for growfast.sh and the reply it drafted for each. Don't post anything.`
5. `Listen on Reddit for people who need <my product> (<domain>, <one sentence>). Keywords: cold email, lead generation, find customers. Tell me first if this would replace an existing monitor.`
